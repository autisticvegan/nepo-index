      How This Works

      Every entry was researched and then independently fact-checked by a second pass that tried to knock it down. Anything that couldn’t be confirmed got cut. Each entry links its sources; if you spot an error, the sources are right there to check against.

      The Nepo Score (0–100) adds up four things: Leg-up (0–40, how directly family produced the outcome), Prize (0–30, a country beats a company beats a job), Brazenness (0–20, dad buying the team scores higher than a famous surname), and Irony (0–10, denials, rule changes, satire of one’s own situation).

      Spoon tiers borrow from Korea’s “spoon class theory” (수저계급론): bronze, silver, gold, diamond.

      What this is not: an accusation of wrongdoing. Plenty of people on this list are talented and work hard. The point is access: who gets the first audition, the first board seat, the first check, or the first country.
